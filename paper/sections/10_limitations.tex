\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Measurement scope.}
The memory result covers post-fit reconstruction only.  Capture-time fitting costs real
compute and requires one full-resolution pass over the images.  \todo{Disclose the upstream
cost table (T-2).}  Workflows that need repeated access to original pixels, for example
relighting, re-editing, or re-fitting at higher budgets, must retain the source data
somewhere.  The result concerns the reconstruction working set and not the deletion of
archives.

\paragraph{Teacher ceiling.}
Compact supervision can at best reproduce what the captures preserved.  Fit quality bounds
reconstruction quality, and high-frequency texture beyond the component budget is
unrecoverable downstream.  The parity question is therefore jointly a question about
capture budgets, and the compression analysis of \cref{fig:rd} is part of the result's
support.

\paragraph{View-dependent appearance.}
Current captures store one color model per 2D component fitted from one photograph.  The
refinement trains SH0, and incremental SH3 was immaterial under compact supervision on one
scene (\cref{tab:ablations}).  Strongly view-dependent scenes with specular or transparent
materials may expose this as the binding constraint.  \todo{Demonstrate SH growth under
compact supervision on a specular scene or scope the result to approximately diffuse
captures (T-11).}

\paragraph{Geometry guarantees.}
The containment invariant of \cref{sec:containment} is a visual-hull center rule.  It
cannot detect hull-interior floaters and does not prove surface occupancy.
\todo{Establish an independent occupancy test (T-12).}

\paragraph{Gauge non-identifiability.}
The amplitude and color gauge freedom of the captures (\cref{sec:field-model}) means some
2D quantities have no physical 3D reading.  Every stage that consumes raw amplitudes must
be gauge-audited, and \cref{sec:refinement} explains which repairs this rules out.

\paragraph{Association risk.}
Covariance intersection is conservative by construction, so the principal failure mode of
the tomographic initializer is false contributor association across views, for example on
repetitive texture and thin structures.  The gates of \cref{eq:gates} are analytic but
heuristic.  The diagnostics of \cref{sec:diagnostics} make failures visible, and the
matched placement control quantifies what the tomographic step adds.

\paragraph{Evidence maturity.}
All quantitative statements outside the mechanism results are single-scene,
outcome-exposed development evidence and are labelled as such where they appear.
\todo{The confirmatory program of \cref{sec:todo-list}, with fresh scenes, held-out
cameras, paired seeds, preregistered gates, the controlled resource measurement, standard
benchmarks, and independent audit, is the difference between this draft and a submittable
paper.}

\paragraph{Static scenes.}
The pipeline is static by design.  The carrier concept extends naturally toward dynamic
capture, from per-frame 2D Gaussian video over space-time beam fusion to 4D carriers and
standard 4D Gaussian splatting.  This paper establishes the static foundation and defers
temporal modeling.

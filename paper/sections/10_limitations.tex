\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The memory result covers post-fit reconstruction only.  Capture-time fitting costs real
compute and requires one full-resolution pass over the images.  \todo{Disclose the
upstream cost table, see T-2.}  Workflows which need repeated access to the original
pixels, for example relighting, re-editing or re-fitting at higher budgets, must retain
the source data somewhere.  The result concerns the reconstruction working set and not the
deletion of archives.

Compact supervision can at best reproduce what the captures preserved.  Fit quality
bounds reconstruction quality, and high-frequency texture beyond the component budget is
unrecoverable downstream.  The parity question is thus jointly a question about capture
budgets, and the compression analysis of \cref{fig:rd} is part of the result's support.

The current captures store one color model per 2D component fitted from one photograph.
The refinement trains SH0, and incremental SH3 was immaterial under compact supervision
on one scene, see \cref{tab:ablations}.  Strongly view-dependent scenes with specular or
transparent materials may expose this as the binding constraint.  \todo{Demonstrate SH
growth under compact supervision on a specular scene or scope the result to approximately
diffuse captures, see T-11.}

The containment invariant of \cref{sec:containment} is a visual-hull center rule.  It
cannot detect hull-interior floaters and does not prove surface occupancy.
\todo{Establish an independent occupancy test, see T-12.}  Due to the amplitude and color
gauge freedom of the captures, see \cref{sec:gauge}, some 2D quantities have no physical
3D reading, and every stage which consumes raw amplitudes must be gauge-audited.

Covariance intersection is conservative by construction, so the principal failure mode of
the tomographic initializer is false contributor association across views, for example on
repetitive texture and thin structures.  The gates of \cref{eq:gates} are analytic but
heuristic.  The diagnostics of \cref{sec:diagnostics} make failures visible, and the
matched placement control quantifies what the tomographic step adds.

All quantitative statements outside the mechanism results are single-scene,
outcome-exposed development evidence and are labelled as such where they appear.
\todo{The confirmatory program of \cref{sec:todo-list}, with fresh scenes, held-out
cameras, paired seeds, preregistered gates, the controlled resource measurement, standard
benchmarks and independent audit, is the difference between this draft and a submittable
paper.}

The pipeline is static by design.  The carrier concept extends naturally toward dynamic
capture, from per-frame 2D Gaussian video over space-time beam fusion to 4D carriers and
standard 4D Gaussian splatting.  This paper establishes the static foundation and defers
temporal modeling.

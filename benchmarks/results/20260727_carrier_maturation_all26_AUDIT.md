## Major findings

### Four maturation boundaries did not converge

Fixed topology and the final standard settle satisfy the frozen train-PSNR plateau rule. Clone
recoveries 1–3 and higher-SH consume their full 15,000-update safety budgets without satisfying
it. The raw stage IDs were minted before the phase outcomes were known and contain the suffix
`_converged`; they are retained as artifact identifiers only. The comparison manifest and
browser page now call them cap endpoints and include an explicit erratum.

This falsifies the process-level proposition that the frozen all-26 schedule achieved
convergence-between-stages. It does not make the final fitted reconstruction invalid.

### Sequential gains are not causal stage effects

The immediate clone boundaries reduce native PSNR, while each following recovery finishes above
its pre-clone boundary. Because the design has no matched no-clone continuation and recovery time
is inseparable from each wave, neither the rebound nor the final endpoint can be attributed to
cloning. The same limitation applies to repair-stage before/after deltas without matched
ablations.

### Selection and evaluation are fully in-sample

The exact fit, selection, and reporting sets all contain the same 26 cameras and the held-out set
is empty. Per-view arithmetic and source bindings are correct, but they measure training-scene
reconstruction only. The free-orbit animation is a qualitative handoff without ground truth.

### Capacity saturates early

Standard growth reaches the 100,000-Gaussian hard cap at the first 5,000-update checkpoint and
remains there. Final direct carrier survival is 59.50%, while 88.78% of original roots retain at
least one descendant. These are valid lineage diagnostics, not evidence that preservation or
growth caused quality.

### Restart chronology limits evidence class

V1 failed before model-quality outcomes. A first V2 process then exposed Beam/repair outcomes and
a step-5,000 checkpoint before the PyCharm crash. The canonical run is a fresh exact V2 restart:
the audit proves identical protocol, implementation hashes, seed, and inputs, and the partial
bundle remains preserved. This supports integrity but not confirmatory independence.

## Audit verdict

The artifact bundle is internally coherent, replayably bound, finite, and suitable for
development inspection. Its final quantitative endpoint may be reported only as an all-fitted,
single-scene, single-seed reconstruction result. The frozen convergence-between-stages proposition
is rejected for this execution because four required phases hit their caps.

A decisive successor needs non-outcome-exposed scenes, held-out cameras, multiple paired seeds,
matched no-clone and repair controls, a policy for cap exhaustion, actual Original-3DGS and
compressed-SfM baselines, and a compact-only optimization arm. Performance claims additionally
need controlled repeats and an idle-GPU protocol. Until then, keep the implementation opt-in,
change no default, and promote no paper claim.

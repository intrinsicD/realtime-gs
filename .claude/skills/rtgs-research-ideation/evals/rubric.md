# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension 0-2:

- Repository grounding: uses current code, negative results, and evidence boundaries.
- Diversity: preserves independent recombination, assumption, primitive, evidence, and transfer
  lanes.
- Transfer rigor: names preserved mechanism, broken correspondence, invention, barrier, and
  counter-analogy.
- Novelty calibration: searches primary sources, records cutoff, and avoids absolute claims.
- Falsifiability: every surviving ambitious idea has a cheap killing test and abandonment rule.
- Workflow routing: selected work enters the task and experiment contracts before execution.

A passing response scores at least 9/12 and receives no zero in novelty calibration,
falsifiability, or workflow routing.
